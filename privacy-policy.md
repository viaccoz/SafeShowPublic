# Safe Show Privacy Policy

Safe Show respects your privacy. The application does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, nor does it upload the media (images, PDFs, text) you share with it. All content processing happens entirely locally on your device.

The app uses third-party services which may collect basic diagnostic and usage data according to their respective privacy policies:
* Google AdMob: For displaying banner ads (unless upgraded to premium).
* Google Play Billing: For processing the premium upgrade.
